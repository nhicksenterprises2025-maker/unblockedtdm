FIRST CONTACT — PLAYABLE TUTORIAL

Brand-new pilots are offered a short training run before the real game. It teaches movement → auto-fire → Shards/Core → DASH → OVERDRIVE → Anchor capture using the actual controls. The tutorial gives no Credits, score records or Mastery, so replay it whenever you want.

CONTROLS — TOUCH + DESKTOP

iPhone/iPad: drag anywhere to move, then use the on-screen DASH and OVERDRIVE buttons. Desktop Chrome/Edge: WASD or Arrow Keys move, your mouse sets auto-fire target priority, SPACE activates DASH, E activates OVERDRIVE, ESC pauses, F toggles fullscreen, and 1/2/3 choose upgrade cards. Auto-fire remains automatic on both platforms.

THE BASIC LOOP

Move with the controls for your platform. Your weapon automatically fires. On desktop, the mouse reticle biases auto-fire toward the enemy nearest the cursor; on touch, the nearest valid target is used. Collect cyan Rift Shards to fill the CORE meter and choose Evolutions. DASH is your emergency movement/invulnerability tool. OVERDRIVE is a temporary damage/fire-rate/speed burst that must be earned between uses.

WHAT YOU ARE TRYING TO DO

There is no finish line: survive as long as possible, score higher, complete Mastery Directives, earn Rift Credits, improve weapon Mastery and Hangar systems, and learn each threat. The game intentionally becomes more hostile the longer and stronger your run gets.

IMPORTANT TERMS

COREYour run level. Pick up Shards; when the requirement fills, choose 1 of 3 Evolutions.
T# / THREATHow aggressively the Rift is scaling against your build. Higher Threat means tougher/faster enemies, more pressure and better score value.
RELICRare, powerful run-only upgrade. Relics are stronger than Evolutions.
RELIC STRAINThe cost of stacking Relics. After your first two, extra Relics weaken healing, raise incoming damage and accelerate Threat.
TRAUMATemporary anti-heal after you take damage. Healing works worse while Trauma is active.
SIPHONEDA Siphon enemy or Leeching Warden is suppressing your repairs. Kill/escape the source.
BACKLASHTemporary spawn-pressure punishment caused by risky choices or failed objectives.
RIFT SURGEShort high-pressure event: more enemies and faster Overdrive gain.
BREAKPOINTThe permanent 50-second milestone. Clearing it banks bonus progression.

ARENA EVENTS

ANCHORCyan zone. Stand inside for 3 seconds before its timer expires. Success banks a Credit + Shards; failure ruptures and causes Backlash.
BREACHPink portal core. Auto-fire can target it. Destroy it before the timer expires or it erupts and spawns reinforcements.
BLACKOUTVisibility and auto-target range collapse temporarily. DASH creates a pulse scan; OVERDRIVE illuminates a much larger area.
FRACTURERed telegraphed blast zone. It hurts you AND enemies, so you can bait enemies into it.

HUNGER — WHAT 1.8 ACTUALLY ADDED

There is no hunger meter on your character. HUNGER refers to the Devourer enemy. A Devourer prioritizes loose Rift Shards on the floor. Every Shard it eats makes it bigger, faster, tougher and more valuable. After it eats at least 3 Shard-weight it becomes GORGED, stops scavenging and aggressively hunts you with radial attacks. Kill the Gorged Devourer to recover what it stole, get extra Shard value and bank +1 Rift Credit.

MAJOR ENEMIES

STALKERFast miniboss. Appears early and periodically; can award Relics.
WARDENMajor boss built around telegraphed aimed volleys and radial novas. Modifiers change its behavior.
HARVESTERAlternating major boss. Creates Gravity Wells and pull pulses that change your movement routes.
NEMESISAdaptive miniboss triggered by high Threat/strong builds. Teleports and escalates below half HP.

BOSS SPOILS

After a Warden/Harvester kill you choose: RELIC SIGNAL for immediate run power; BANK SALVAGE for +3 Credits and +5 weapon Mastery XP; or FEED THE CORE for +5 Shards/+800 score but immediate Backlash.

CONTRACTS — 2.2

Operations now have Tier I → Tier II → Tier III. Clear a tier once to unlock the next tier for that Operation. Every tier shows its exact objective, modifier and base payout before launch. The first clear of a tier adds a one-time bonus. When the objective completes, your remaining HP and any failed Anchors/Breaches produce a C / B / A / S grade; B adds +1 Credit/+1 XP, A +2/+2, S +3/+3. Your best grade is saved permanently. FREE RUN is unchanged and has no Contract modifier.

PERMANENT PROGRESSION

Rift Credits buy Hangar upgrades. Weapon Mastery XP increases one weapon's Rank and small permanent damage bonus; veteran ranks also require best-run survival times. Mastery Directives are optional per-run bonus-XP targets, not requirements to rank up.
