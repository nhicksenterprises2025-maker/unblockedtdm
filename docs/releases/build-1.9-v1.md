# UnblockedTDM Build 1.9 — Version 1

## Release Candidate / Gameplay HUD

Build 1.9 Version 1 is the feature-complete release-candidate pass before 2.0. It preserves canonical gameplay balance while rebuilding the live match HUD toward the approved top-down shooter reference.

### Gameplay HUD overhaul
- Compact top-center Blue / timer / Red scoreboard strip.
- Circular top-left minimap with stronger bezel and directional presentation.
- Cleaner bottom-left Health and Stamina modules.
- Consolidated bottom-right weapon/ammo presentation with clearer active-slot treatment.
- Dash charges presented as a compact tactical resource block above the weapon panel.
- Stronger central round countdown / Sudden Death card.
- More deliberate combat/streak banners.
- Debug-only helper text stays hidden during normal play.
- Tighter desktop spacing and stronger hierarchy across the live HUD.

### Release-candidate goals
- Preserve the complete 3v3 match flow from 1.8.
- Preserve settings, custom keybinds, 25 persistent loadouts and last-match persistence.
- Preserve postgame, Rematch and Main Menu flow.
- Keep AI/pathfinding, spawn logic and difficulty multipliers unchanged.
- Keep all eight canonical weapon stat blocks unchanged.
- Keep 150 HP, regeneration, movement, sprint and dash rules unchanged.
- Keep 1:30 rounds, first to 12 kills, Sudden Death, first to 5 and maximum 9 rounds unchanged.

### Validation gate
Windows packaging begins only after the complete historical regression chain plus the Build 1.9 HUD/canonical-rule regression passes.
