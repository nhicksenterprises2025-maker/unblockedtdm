# Skirmish Arena 1.97.1 — Phase 5

Phase 5 delivers the fullscreen UI correction and combat VFX pass.

## Fullscreen UI correction
- Reworked the main menu for true 16:9 fullscreen spacing instead of stretching the old windowed layout.
- Rebalanced the PLAY, LOADOUTS, WEAPON INFO, SETTINGS and QUIT hierarchy.
- Corrected conflicting HUD anchors and safe-zone offsets.
- Repositioned and resized the minimap, health, stamina, dash, weapon HUD and top match score for fullscreen.
- Improved fullscreen scaling for Settings, Loadouts, Pause, Postgame, Tactical Map, Scoreboard and between-round loadout selection.
- Added responsive handling for large 1080p+ displays, 1280/1366-class displays and shorter-height screens.

## Combat VFX pass
- Stronger weapon muzzle glow and muzzle flash feedback.
- Improved bullet and shot impact bursts.
- Dedicated critical-hit impact treatment.
- Improved launcher explosion flash, shockwave, debris and layered blast effects.
- Preserved existing tracers, smoke, sparks, damage numbers, hitmarkers and screen shake.
- Eliminated players leave short-lived visible body/remnant feedback before fully clearing.

## Gameplay safety
- No weapon damage, fire rate, spread, reload, ammo or movement balance changes.
- No AI behavior changes.
- No map geometry, collision or spawn changes.
- No match-rule changes.
