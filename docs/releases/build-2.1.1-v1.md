# Skirmish Arena 2.1.1

## 1000-Level Account Career

2.1.1 replaces the original prototype Career progression with the full long-term account system.

### Career
- Career cap expanded from 100 to **1,000 levels**.
- **26 permanent ranks** from Recruit I through Omnipotent.
- Each rank ships with its own authored Skirmish Arena emblem and material identity.
- The full Career requires **4,137,375 XP** to reach Level 1000.
- Career completion is measured by total XP rather than level number.
- The existing home Career strip now opens the dedicated Career interface without changing the approved main-menu tile layout.

### Match XP
- Kill: **8 XP**
- Assist: **4 XP**
- Round win: **16 XP**
- Round loss: **4 XP**
- Match win: **100 XP**
- Postgame now shows the complete XP breakdown before applying Career progress.
- Multi-level gains are summarized as one level jump instead of forcing one animation per level.

### Permanent Milestones
Five lifetime milestone tracks progress automatically:
- Kills
- Assists
- Round Wins
- Match Wins
- Matches Completed

There are no daily, weekly or rotating Career challenges. The five milestone tracks award a combined **880,000 Career XP** across their full lifetime progression.

### Career Interface
The dedicated Career screen contains only three sections:
- **Overview** — current emblem, level, rank, XP, XP-weighted Career completion, play time and lifetime record.
- **Ranks** — all 26 Career emblems with current, earned and locked states.
- **Milestones** — only the next target for each of the five permanent tracks.

Rank changes receive a dedicated **Rank Promotion** presentation. Level 1000 unlocks **Omnipotent**, the final account Career rank.

### Existing Account Data
Existing lifetime match statistics are preserved when moving from the prototype Career system. Prototype Career XP is not carried into the new 1–1000 economy, so the new Career begins from its intended progression baseline.

### Gameplay
No weapon balance, movement, AI, map, spawn or match-rule values were changed in this update.
