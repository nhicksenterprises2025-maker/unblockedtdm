# Skirmish Arena 2.0

Skirmish Arena 2.0 is the first major progression-focused release. It keeps the existing competitive gameplay intact while adding persistent career progression so every completed match contributes to a long-term profile.

## Career Progression
- Added a persistent 100-level career system.
- Every completed match awards Career XP.
- XP rewards match completion, wins, kills, assists, damage, critical hits and best streak performance.
- Career progression is stored locally and survives game/launcher restarts and future launcher updates.
- Added eight non-power career titles: Recruit, Skirmisher, Operator, Vanguard, Elite, Veteran, Ace and Arena Legend.
- Career titles unlock at Levels 1, 5, 10, 20, 35, 50, 75 and 100.
- Progression does not modify damage, health, movement, weapons, AI or any other competitive gameplay value.

## Persistent Career Stats
- Tracks lifetime matches, wins and losses.
- Tracks lifetime kills, deaths and assists.
- Tracks lifetime damage and critical hits.
- Tracks lifetime best kill streak.
- Tracks lifetime K/D and win rate.
- Stores the 20 most recent completed matches with K/D/A, result and XP earned.

## Main Menu Career Strip
- Added a compact Career section to the existing home screen without adding another large navigation card.
- Shows current level and title.
- Shows career win/loss record and win rate.
- Shows lifetime K/D, kills and match count.
- Shows current XP progress and the next career-title milestone.
- Shows the five most recent match results when vertical space allows.

## Postgame Progression
- Every completed match now shows a dedicated Career XP result panel.
- Shows total XP earned for the match.
- Shows an XP breakdown for completion, victory, kills, assists, damage, critical hits and streak performance.
- Shows level progress after the match.
- Clearly identifies level-ups and newly unlocked career titles.

## 2.0 Competitive Contract
- No weapon damage or handling changes.
- No health changes.
- No sprint, movement or dash changes.
- No AI difficulty or behavior changes.
- No Training Complex geometry, collision or spawn changes.
- No round timer, kill target or first-to-five match-rule changes.

## Foundation Preserved from the 1.99 RC
- Professional Phase 8 HUD and launcher presentation remain intact.
- Reliable hash-backed launcher update detection remains intact.
- Phase 9 30 Hz minimap optimization remains intact.
- Phase 9 hidden-window render protection and focus-loss auto-pause remain intact.
- Phase 9 pre-2.0 local-data migration snapshots remain intact.
