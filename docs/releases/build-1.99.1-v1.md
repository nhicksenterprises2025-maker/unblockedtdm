# Skirmish Arena 1.99.1 — Phase 7

Phase 7 gives Training Complex a dedicated visual/readability pass while preserving its existing competitive geometry and gameplay contracts.

## Training Complex Visual Overhaul
- Added a brighter central combat-space lighting treatment and subtle team-color spawn-zone ambience.
- Added clearer BLUE SPAWN, RED SPAWN, NORTH WAREHOUSE and SOUTH WAREHOUSE landmark presentation.
- Added a central TC // MID floor landmark for faster orientation.
- Added Skirmish Arena branding to terminal structures.
- Added training markings and identity details to crates, barriers and warehouse structures.
- Improved cover readability with additional structure outlines and presentation detail.
- Added matching BLUE / MID / RED landmark cues to the full tactical map and a Training Complex marker to the minimap.

## Compatibility / Scope
- Training Complex collision and map geometry are unchanged.
- Spawn coordinates and lane geometry are unchanged.
- Enemy tactical reveal remains limited to 1.5 seconds after enemy fire.
- Weapon stats, movement, sprint, dash, AI, combat and match rules are unchanged.
- Phase 3 tactical HUD, Phase 4 audio/Auto Sprint, Phase 5 VFX and Phase 6 menu rebuild remain intact.
