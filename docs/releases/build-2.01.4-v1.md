# Skirmish Arena 2.01.4

2.01.4 fixes Weapon Info scrolling without changing the one-page Loadouts layout introduced in 2.01.3.

## Weapon Info Scroll Repair
- Weapon Info now uses a real vertically scrollable content column inside the fixed main-menu shell.
- The Weapon Info page can be scrolled normally with the mouse wheel when its content is taller than the viewport.
- Horizontal scrolling remains disabled.
- Main-menu scrolling remains available normally.
- Loadouts remains a one-page, no-vertical-scroll screen.

## Compatibility / Scope
- Weapon models and the gameplay crosshair spread preview remain unchanged from 2.01.2/2.01.3.
- No weapon stats, accuracy, movement, AI, spawn, map geometry or match rules changed.
